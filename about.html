About MANASA CAFE

Welcome to MANASA CAFE — a luxury open-space beachside haven where the comforting aroma of freshly roasted specialty coffee meets the soothing rhythm of ocean waves and golden sunset horizons.

Set against the picturesque coastline, our outdoor seating area features natural wooden tables, warm seaside breezes, lush palm trees, and cozy ambient fairy lights. Whether you're catching the morning sea breeze with an artisan espresso or enjoying a peaceful sunset evening with handcrafted desserts, MANASA CAFE is your tranquil escape by the beach.

Guiding Philosophy

Our Pillars of Excellence

Every cup poured, every pastry baked, and every smile shared follows our four timeless commitments.

Direct Trade Sourcing

We work hand-in-hand with multi-generational coffee farmers, paying above fair-trade prices to ensure sustainable practices and the freshest harvest.

Ocean Care & Eco-Conscious

We preserve our pristine beach with 100% biodegradable takeaway packaging, zero single-use plastics, and regular coastal cleanup initiatives.

Handcrafted Daily

From slow-fermented sourdough loaves and French butter croissants to fresh cold-pressed mocktails, everything is made fresh from scratch every morning.
